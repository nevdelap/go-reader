Welcome to 語 Reader 1

A free Japanese reader for advanced learners. Type or paste Japanese text from AI tools, websites, or learning materials, then clicktap any morpheme to see its reading and English meaning. ClickTap the E.G. button to try the app with sample text and explore its features.

  • No AI — Dictionary lookups happen locally in your browser, fast and private
  • Grammar morphemes — Toggle dimming for enhanced learning
  • Keyboard shortcuts — Ctrl+K to clear text, Ctrl+V to paste, Alt+D to toggle reading direction, Enter in panel to enlarge morpheme to clearly see kanji form and distinguish dakuten and handakuten
  • Everything stays in your browser — No login, no server, no ads, no tracking 2
  1. When you are speaking English, pronounce the name like the English verb "to go" — "Go Reader".
  2. It just counts users by country so that I can see how much use it is getting.
